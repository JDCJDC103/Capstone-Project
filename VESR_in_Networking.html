VESR in Networking

VESR when it pertains to Netowrking as a whole and the project that our team is working on remains an extremely important system of beliefs. Our project is to work on a company’s external firewall systems and add traffic blocks that prevent access to the company’s network.

The core values in place when working on this project need to correspond with the goals and aspirations of Ritedose. The company creates medical vials in mass quantities, spreading important medication. Any safe and secure webpage that workers may need access to such as medical sites should remain available.

Ethical considerations come up in the decisions of what to block and what not to block. There are many safe websites that do not pertain to the employee’s work. Examples of this, if they aren’t against company policy, are websites that are for entertainment such as youtube or social media platforms. While these are not used to aid in professional work. Employees on break may want a way to rest through the usage of entertainment platforms. It needs to be asked if every single link to a page not used for work needs to be blocked so that employees can have a more relaxing break. Social media is another ethical consideration. Most people use social media as recreational and not as an important messaging system. I know of people that utilize it to communicate with loved ones. During break or even outside of it, perhaps some social media platforms need not be stifled through the firewall systems.

It is our social responsibility to balance company wants, employee needs, and to create a pleasant working environment through the blocks or lack there of when doing our work. Values and ethics in business need to focus on not just the security of the company’s network, but also the health and wants of the employees while giving the clients what they want for us to accomplish.
