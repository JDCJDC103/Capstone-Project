What is VESR?

Values Ethics and Social Responsibility (VESR) are the core beliefs, moral choices, and level of accountability that needs to be taken when operating in a business environment.

Values are the principles that you build your strategy on which will serve as the central guide during the operation of the company, or your operational strategies when completing work as a part of a business. Without strong moral values, companies and workers can find themselves building a poor reputation without a guiding force to steer them in a more successful way.

Ethics are the decisions that you make when moral quandaries appear while you are involved with your day to day activities. Ethics when it pertains to business activities begs the question of which action that can be taken in the line of work will be positive for humankind. In other terms, business should not solely be built upon profits and be ruled by monetary growth but to also hold a goal of bettering other people.

Ethics Examples:

  1. Advertisements should be truthful about the products rather than hiding or falsifying information.
  2. Employee religious beliefs should be respected and not cause the employees to be treated differently.
  3. Working conditions deemed unsafe should be made less dangerous.
  4. The company should choose to spend their money reducing the amount of waste product it produces.
